Privacy Policy

Memo — Voice Dictation  ·  Last updated: May 2026

Short version: Memo records your voice only when you activate it. Audio is sent to OpenAI for transcription and then discarded. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data.

1. What Memo does

Memo is a macOS dictation app. When you hold the global shortcut (⌥ Space), it records your voice, sends the audio to OpenAI's Whisper API for transcription, and pastes the resulting text into whatever app you are using.

2. Data we collect

Memo does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Specifically:

  • Audio recordings — created in memory only while you hold the hotkey. The file is deleted from disk immediately after transcription.
  • Transcribed text — stored locally in an optional history list inside macOS sandboxed storage (UserDefaults). It never leaves your device unless you copy-paste it yourself.
  • API keys — stored exclusively in the macOS Keychain on your device. They are never transmitted to any server other than OpenAI and Anthropic/Claude (if AI post-processing is enabled).

3. Third-party services

When you transcribe audio, your recording is sent to OpenAI via their Whisper API. If you enable AI post-processing, transcribed text is sent to Anthropic (Claude API) or OpenAI (GPT API). These requests are made directly from your device using your own API key. No intermediary server is involved.

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Privacy Policy

4. Permissions

  • Microphone — required to record your voice. macOS will ask you to grant this permission on first use.
  • Accessibility — required to register the global hotkey and to paste text into other apps by simulating ⌘V. Memo does not read or record keystrokes from other applications.
  • Network — required to reach the OpenAI and/or Anthropic APIs.

5. Analytics & crash reporting

Memo does not include any analytics SDK, crash reporter, or telemetry. No usage data of any kind is sent to the developer.

6. Children

Memo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
